# The EDMA Solid Community of Practice — summary for Sir Tim Berners-Lee and John Bruce

**What it is.** A Community of Practice convened by the EDM Association (EDMA) and co-chaired with the Open Data Institute, pairing the data-management expertise of EDMA's members and authorized partners — the community behind FIBO and the CDMC cloud-data-controls framework, spanning the major banks, asset managers and data vendors — with the Solid project. It is a deliberate two-way exchange: industry brings the requirements, domain models and buying power of regulated sectors; Solid brings the architecture that ends their silos, duplication and custody risk.

**Why it matters.** Regulated buyers will not adopt on promise alone. The group exists to produce the things that turn Solid's model into procurable reality in finance, healthcare and government: sector ontology bindings (FIBO for finance, clinical vocabularies for health), sector-specific security and functional requirements, DPROD-based data-product discovery in pods, reference deployments with named champions, and a vendor-neutral **Solid Certification** — administered by EDMA as neutral certifier, assessed by authorized partners, with the ODI approving the curriculum, so the standard's steward never referees implementations. All outputs are published openly under MIT.

**How it runs.** Launching as a forum, converting to a formal working group in September 2026 with defined deliverables. Co-chairs: Jim Halcomb (EDMA), Jacobus Geluk (agnos.ai), Roberto Breitman and Jesse Wright (ODI); FIBO/ontology leads Shaun Rolls and Elisa Kendall; sector leads drawn from LSEG, VITO and others. The community is hosted on EDM Connect with a public Solid-branded microsite; participation is open and vendor-neutral, decisions by consensus.

**Where you come in.** The 14 July launch webinar — your fireside conversation — is the group's public starting gun. What the audience of data-management leaders needs from you is the *why*: one copy of the truth, data that travels under its owner's control, and an invitation to build the sector deployments this group will certify. The charter (shared alongside this note) welcomes your input, particularly on the framing of Solid's role for enterprise.

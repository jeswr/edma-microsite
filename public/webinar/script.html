ODI Stewardship

EMMA (~1 min): "A quick word on who we are. The ODI is a mission-driven non-profit, founded in 2012 by Sir Tim Berners-Lee and Sir Nigel Shadbolt, building open and trustworthy data ecosystems. Since October 2024, the ODI has stewarded Solid: its standards at the W3C, its codebases, and its global community. Its stewardship aims to establish ethical data governance, addressing privacy, transparency and control so data can be used responsibly for individuals, society and the economy. Our work spans the clients and partners you see here, alongside practical impact in training, open banking and support for early-stage organisations. 5

Data Spectrum (Full Bleed)

EMMA — bridge into Solid (~45s): "One idea that shapes our work is the Data Spectrum. Data isn't simply open or closed — it runs from closed, through shared, to open. Most valuable data sits in the shared middle: it can't be fully open, but locking it away wastes its value. Solid is precisely how that shared middle becomes practical — data that moves between organisations while staying under the control of the person or organisation it describes. Jesse, over to you."

6

Divider · Solid

Transition slide. Emma hands to Jesse for a concise introduction to Solid: what it is, why the current app-centric data model breaks down, and the live walkthrough.

7

What Is Solid

JESSE (~1.5 min): "I'm Jesse Wright, Solid Project Lead at the ODI. One sentence: Solid is an open standard for user-centric data and identity on the web. Today every app keeps its own copy of your data; Solid inverts that. Your data lives in a Pod, and AI agents and apps read and write only with your permission, at the level of individual items. Solid extends the web architecture we already trust: identity, authorization and a universal data API layered on top of existing web standards. Open linked-data standards mean the same data is reused everywhere without custom integration, always under the control of whoever it describes. Privacy by design, built on standard web tech from Tim's group at MIT, stewarded by the ODI, standardised at the W3C."

8

The Problem

JESSE (~1.5 min): "The same data is collected, copied and secured many times over, yet is rarely current, portable or under the control of the person or organisation it describes. The result is familiar: silos and duplication, more places for data to be breached, vendor lock-in, all-or-nothing consent, and having to prove your identity from scratch at every institution. Solid fixes this by decoupling data from applications. Services read what they're allowed to use from the person's or organisation's Pod, instead of keeping another copy. As AI agents act for us, they need current data the person can still control — Solid gives them a way to use it with permission."

13

What We'Re Building

JIM & ELISA (~1 min): Jim — "A community of practice is simply a neutral space where the people with the problem and the people building the technology solve it together. Ours brings the EDM Association's members — the data-management leaders of finance, healthcare and government — together with the Solid community. We do three concrete things: real pilots per sector, connecting Solid to the models each sector already relies on — starting with the Financial Industry Business Ontology, or FIBO, in finance — and independent certification. We'll go one level deeper on each."

14

Focus A · Pilots

JIM (~1 min): "The first workstream is real deployments. We want end-to-end pilots that can be shown, tested and written up as reference implementations, with named champions, case studies and reports, and webinars so others can follow. Finance goes first around customer onboarding and lending decisions: data checked against the customer's own Pod, reusable identity and eligibility checks, and credentials issued by a bank that the customer can use elsewhere. Then healthcare and government apply the same pattern where repeated checks and copied records create cost and risk, starting with practical services that can be demonstrated."

15

Focus B · Standards

ELISA (~1 min): "The second workstream is standards alignment. Solid should not ask each industry to throw away the data definitions it already trusts. In finance, that means mapping the Financial Industry Business Ontology (FIBO) to Solid so data in a Pod means the same thing to every participating financial institution. We also need Pods to describe the data products they contain and how access should be requested. Healthcare and government follow the same pattern."

16

Focus C · Certification

JIM (~1 min): "The third workstream is certification. We want to adapt the EDM Council's Cloud Data Management Capabilities (CDMC) controls to Solid services, so Pod providers and application teams know what evidence is expected for governance, access control and operational readiness. A Solid-CDMC certification would confirm that a Solid service meets those adapted controls. The neutral mark should show that Solid services are fit for deployment in a particular sector, including security, conformance and sector-specific requirements. Assessment can run through EDMA's established authorised-partner model, with trained partners assessing Solid services and a neutral body overseeing certification across sectors."

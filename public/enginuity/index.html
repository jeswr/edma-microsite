What is Solid

An open W3C standard from Sir Tim Berners-Lee. Your data lives in a Pod you control; apps and institutions read and write only with permission.

No silos, no stale copies, no lock-in.

Stewarded by the ODI

Since 2024 the Open Data Institute stewards Solid: its standards at the W3C, its codebases, and its global community.

Founded by Sir Tim Berners-Lee and Sir Nigel Shadbolt.

Finance-ready, with EDMA

With the EDM Association we run the Solid Community of Practice: binding FIBO to Solid, setting sector requirements, and building the Solid Certification.

Verified lending data, reusable KYC, bank-provided identity.

“The emergence of the latest generative AI, indeed all of modern machine learning, depends on vast amounts of data. Without data, there would be no AI.”

AI systems depend heavily on high-quality, well-governed data. But those building, using, assuring or regulating AI often focus on the models rather than on the data that shapes them.

We risk overlooking a core point: without secure, reliable AI-ready data foundations that are accessible to everyone in the same way, no AI system will truly serve the public.

The ODI's Data-centric AI (DCAI) programme tackles these challenges and their implications for healthy, trustworthy AI ecosystems. We study causes, question assumptions, unpack misleading trends, and build critical components of the public data infrastructure for AI.

IDEA
An Interchange for Data and Enterprise AI

We're launching IDEA, an interchange for data and enterprise AI, with SAP providing founding sponsorship.

IDEA will set up a programme of research, community building, and peer learning to help enterprises of all sizes make the most of the promise AI holds through AI-ready data infrastructure, standards, and well-governed enterprise data.
